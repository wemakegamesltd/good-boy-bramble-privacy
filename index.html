Privacy Policy

Good Boy, Bramble (Android) by WeMakeGames. Last updated: 29 October 2026

Good Boy, Bramble is a sheep-herding game. We built it so that it does not collect any information about you. This page explains what that means.

We don't collect or share any data

The game doesn't collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information or usage data. It has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools. The app does not even have permission to use the internet.

In-app purchase

The game offers one optional in-app purchase, which opens all the farms. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play under its own terms and privacy policy. We never see your payment details. The game only learns whether the purchase has been made, and keeps that on your device.

Your progress stays on your device

Your progress, settings, outfits and sheep album are saved only on your phone or tablet, inside the app's own storage. We can't see them. If you uninstall the app, they are deleted. If Android's own backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this save data in your personal device backup, which is managed by Google under your Google account. We never receive it.

Photos and clips

If you choose to save a photo or clip from the game, it is saved to your device's own gallery (in a "Good Boy Bramble" folder). It isn't uploaded anywhere. What you do with it after that is up to you.

Notifications are optional

"Gentle reminders" are off unless you turn them on. If you do, the game asks for permission to show notifications and then shows at most one friendly note a day. These reminders are scheduled on your device. No information is sent anywhere to make them work. You can turn them off in the game or in your device's settings at any time.

Vibration

The game uses short vibrations during play. This needs no personal information.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because we don't collect any personal information from anyone, we don't collect any from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
